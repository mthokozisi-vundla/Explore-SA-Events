About Us

Explore SA Events is a website that helps people find events in South Africa. We share music festivals, food markets, sports and cultural events in one place.

Our Story

Good events can be hard to find. We made this website as a group project to make it easy for everyone to see what is happening and plan their time.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To help everyone find and enjoy events in South Africa.

Our Vision

To be the best place to discover South African events.
